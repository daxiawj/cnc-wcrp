  • 7th SPARC General Assembly, 24-28 October 2022, on a multi-hub basis with three locations (hubs) : the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR) in Boulder (USA), the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) in Reading (UK) and the First Institute of Oceanography (FIO) in Qingdao (China).
