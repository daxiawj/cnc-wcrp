 Published: 02 June 2022

The Surface Ocean–Lower Atmosphere Study (SOLAS), an international and interdisciplinary research project on biogeochemical-physical air-sea interactions and processes will hold its 8th SOLAS Open Science Conference on 25-29 September 2022 in Cape Town, South Africa (hybrid format). The registration, abstract & discussion session proposal submission, travel fund application, and Early Career Scientist Day (ECSD) application are open until 10 June 2022.

  • To learn more, visit the 8th SOLAS Open Science Conference website
